01 / Clinical cohorts

Integrated cancer and cardiovascular risk

Cleaning and validating EMR data on 140,827 patients and 549,713 imaging scans. Developing a multi-disease risk model combining BCSC breast cancer and PCE ASCVD estimates through fixed-time survival and joint-probability methods, with subgroup analysis.

02 / Clinical data quality

Diagnosis validation across sources

Investigating missingness, outliers, and discordant diagnoses through Epic chart review and cross-checks of pathology reports, ICD-10 codes, and tumor registry records to produce analysis-ready datasets.

EpicQA/QCClinical informatics
03 / Longitudinal neuroscience

Brain connectivity over time

Preprocessing and harmonizing approximately 25,000 functional-connectivity matrices across baseline, two-year, and four-year visits, supporting longitudinal analysis of clinical phenotypes.

Python + RLongitudinal dataNeuroimaging
02 / About

At the intersection of data and care.

I am an M.S. student in Biostatistics at Columbia University and a clinical data analyst at NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital / Columbia University. My background in biomedical engineering connects statistical methods with the practical details of how health data are recorded, cleaned, and interpreted.

Across hospital research, medical-device studies, population health, and neuroimaging, I work with clinicians and quantitative collaborators to make analyses reproducible and clinically meaningful.

Interests: biostatistics · real-world evidence · clinical informatics · biomedical machine learning

Genetics course · Individual final project

Epigenetic changes in liver cancer

Integrated TCGA-LIHC RNA-seq and DNA methylation data to examine promoter hypermethylation alongside lower gene expression in hepatocellular carcinoma. The matched multi-omics analysis included 41 patients and identified 108 candidate genes for further study.

P8105 · Group project · 2025

Exploring diabetes risk factors

With a five-person Columbia team, analyzed 253,680 responses from the 2015 U.S. BRFSS health indicators dataset. The project explored behavioral and demographic associations and compared logistic regression, random forest, and XGBoost classification approaches.
